# Color Sources

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500063
- Category: Programming/BASIC

The color sources defined by the [COLOR statement](500068_color_statement.md) and available to various graphic statements are as follows:

0: Background
1: Foreground
2: Multicolor 1
3: Multicolor 2
4: Border (not available to drawing statements)
