# What Is A Demo?

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500010
- Category: Glossary/Demos
- Keywords: Demo

A Demo is a non-interactive or little interacting multimedia presentation made within the computer subculture known as the demoscene, that intends to demonstrate the author's knowledge and skill about programming, drawing, audio composing and designing.

The main Demo's objective is the creation of esthetically pleasing works by overcoming strict technical restrictions.
