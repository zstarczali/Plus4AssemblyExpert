# What Is The Commodore 232?

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500004
- Category: History, Hardware
- Keywords: 232

The Commodore 232 was announced by Commodore, but it never made it to production. It's part of the 264 family ([What Is The Commodore 264 family?](500006_what_is_the_commodore_264_family.md)). However, a very small number of prototypes were manufactured. The casing is virtually identical to the Plus/4's, but it lacks a userport. The machine has 32KB of RAM. A very rare machine to find.
