# FPP

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500214
- Category: Glossary/Demos
- Keywords: fpp,flexible pixel position

FPP stands for Flexible Pixel Position.
Although it may appear to be similar to [FLD](https://plus4world.powweb.com/kb.php?id=500028) and [FPD](https://plus4world.powweb.com/kb.php?id=500213), it's a completely different effect. The two prior effects are just able to 'stretch' the graphics by putting empty space inbetween. With FPP you can put any line of the graphics anywhere on the screen (vertically).
The global stretcher is a further application based on FPP which applies to the whole screen, not to be confused with simple [Stretcher](https://plus4world.powweb.com/kb.php?id=500239), which is based on graphics manipulation.
