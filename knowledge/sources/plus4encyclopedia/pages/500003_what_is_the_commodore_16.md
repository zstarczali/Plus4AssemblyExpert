# What Is The Commodore 16?

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500003
- Category: History, Hardware
- Keywords: 16

The Commodore 16 is also a member of the Commodore 264 family ([What Is The Commodore 264 family?](500006_what_is_the_commodore_264_family.md)). It came in a C=64 style 'breadbox' case, and it had 16KB or RAM. The ROM was identical to the C= Plus/4. The differences - other than the case - are: the C= 16 did not have a User port, and it did not have the 3-Plus-1 software package built in.
