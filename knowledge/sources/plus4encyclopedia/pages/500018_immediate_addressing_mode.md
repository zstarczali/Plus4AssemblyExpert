# Immediate Addressing Mode

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500018
- Category: Programming/Assembly

An example of immediate addressing mode is this: LDA #$34. Our value, $34 is written out specifically, it's "immediately" available.
