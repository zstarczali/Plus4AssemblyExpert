# What Is The Commodore V364?

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500005
- Category: History, Hardware
- Keywords: 364

The Commodore V364 (or 364, Voice 364) was to be the 'deluxe' machine of the 264 family ([What Is The Commodore 264 family?](500006_what_is_the_commodore_264_family.md)). It's primary feature was speech synthesis, using hardware almost identical to the Magic Voice cartridge for the C64. It also featured a numeric keypad.

However, this machine was cancelled before production, and only three prototypes are known to exist.

See also the [Commodore V364](https://plus4world.powweb.com/hardware/Commodore_V364) in the Hardware section.
