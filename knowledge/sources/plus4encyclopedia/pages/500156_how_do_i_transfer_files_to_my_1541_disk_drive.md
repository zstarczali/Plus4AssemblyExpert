# How Do I Transfer Files To My 1541 Disk Drive?

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500156
- Category: Hardware
- Keywords: download, 1541, program, file, copy, disk, drive, transfer

For this purpose, pretend your Plus/4 is a Commodore 64, then visit the following sites:

http://sta.c64.org -- **Star Commander**
http://www.64hdd.com -- **64HDD**
http://www.trikaliotis.net/cbm4win.shtml -- **CBM4Win (Windows NT /2000 / XP)**
http://www.jammingsignal.com/gui4cbm4win/ -- **GUI4CBM4WIN**
http://www.lb.shuttle.de/puffin/cbm4linux/ -- **CBM4Linux (Linux)**

There is no cable to connect a 1551 to a PC, so a 1541 must be used with an adapter cable to the PC. The Star Commander website and the 64HDD website have cables available for purchase.
