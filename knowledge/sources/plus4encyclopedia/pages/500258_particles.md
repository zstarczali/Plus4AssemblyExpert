# Particles

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500258
- Category: Glossary/Demos
- Keywords: particles, Credits 2022-09-13

Particles are transparent or translucent graphic elements, apparently defined as Sprites or, better, [Shadebobs](https://plus4world.powweb.com/kb.php?id=500219), used in both ordered and chaotic systems. A full working particle interacts with another particle summing up their effects, and with a background element by dulling its involved shapes.
