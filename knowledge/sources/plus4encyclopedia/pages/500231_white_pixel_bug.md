# White Pixel Bug

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500231
- Category: Programming/General
- Keywords: white, pixel, bug, raster Credits 2011-11-15 See Also [Raster Split](https://plus4world.powweb.com/kb.php?id=500230)

The White Pixel's Bug is a characteristic bug of TED based machines. It occurs when one color's raster changing is straight displayed on screen away from the beginning of a scanline.
It's basically caused by the latency time the TED needs to pass from the previous assigned color to the new one. It could be only masked but not circumvent.
The so called "white pixel" only looks as "white": the truth is that it's a $FF coloured pixel, hence a green pixel at its higher luminance level.
