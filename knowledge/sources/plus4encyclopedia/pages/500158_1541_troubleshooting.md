**Lights not on**
Make sure you connect the drive to power. Check the fuse. Proper replacement is RadioShack 270-1005 for 110V drives, RadioShack 270-1047 for 220V drives.

If the light on the case (not on the drive mechanism) lights up, you have power to the drive.
