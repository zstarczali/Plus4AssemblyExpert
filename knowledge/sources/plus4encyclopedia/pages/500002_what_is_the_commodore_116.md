# What Is The Commodore 116?

- Source: https://plus4world.powweb.com/plus4encyclopedia/500002
- Category: History, Hardware
- Keywords: 116

The Commodore 116 was another member of the Commodore 264 family ([What Is The Commodore 264 family?](500006_what_is_the_commodore_264_family.md)). It was the original target device of the TED project, which was intended to compete with the cheap Timex-Sinclair computers (its rubber keyboard was actually inspired by the ZX Spectrum's keyboard). It was originally intended to be sold in the US at a price of just $49. In the end, it was released in Germany only - although some stores in Hungary had them available (possibly imported from Germany). It had 16KB RAM, just like the Commodore 16, but it had a much smaller, Plus/4-like box. Its rubber keyboard was difficult to use - most would agree that the keys were a big negative.
